## Field background prompt

Use case: `stylized-concept`. Asset type: 2D game environment background. A cheerful single-screen grassy dinosaur meadow for a gentle preschool game, viewed from a three-quarter top-down perspective similar to a classic cozy farming-game map but with an original design. One continuous enclosed field with soft grass, small wildflower patches, a light dirt path, rounded shrubs, leafy trees, and a low wooden fence. Broad uncluttered playable lawn across the center; clear lower-right space for a separate pond sprite; calm bottom edge for an item tray overlay. Environment only: no dinosaurs, eggs, pond, ball, characters, UI, text, logos, or watermark. Hand-painted children's storybook gouache, warm diffuse morning light, 16:9 fixed camera, full field visible in one screen.
