# First playtest guide

Open the game in a fresh private/incognito browser window and avoid explaining the controls. Sit nearby, but intervene only if the child becomes frustrated.

Observe:

- Does the egg attract a click without prompting?
- Is four taps too many, too few, or satisfying?
- Which parts of the dinosaur are clicked repeatedly?
- Is dragging the ball discovered?
- Is the bath discovered and is the back arrow understood?
- Does the child repeat any sound or animation for fun?
- Does the child name, speak to, or show affection toward the dinosaur?
- Where does attention drop?

Use the circular arrow in the upper-right corner to reset. It must be held for just over one second, preventing accidental resets during normal play.

Do not ask “Do you like it?” during play. Repeated voluntary interaction is the strongest signal.
